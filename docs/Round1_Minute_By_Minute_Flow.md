# Minute-by-Minute Flow: Round 1

Event run sheet for Round 1, transcribed from the planning whiteboard.

## Overview

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Participating teams** | 40 |
| **Competition rooms** | 402, 403 |
| **Chit locations** | Seminar Hall 306 (1st slot), Seminar Hall 406 (2nd slot) |

## Volunteer (APC Member) Allocation

| Assignment | Members | Role |
|------------|---------|------|
| Dynamic POC | 2 | Floating points of contact across venues |
| Seminar Hall 306 / 406 | 3 | Manage the chit locations |
| Room 403 / 402 | 3 per room | 2 on scoresheet, 1 emcee |

## Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Instructions & settlement | 15 min | Teams are seated and briefed on the rules |
| Round starts: solving block 1 | 15 min | Both members work in parallel (see below) |
| Solving block 2 | 15 min | Solving continues |
| Buffer | 20 min | Submission by mail / Google Form, plus chit checking |

**Round duration:** 30 min of solving (2 × 15 min) plus a 20 min buffer, after the 15 min instructions.

> The whiteboard shows "Total Duration: 30 mins + 20 mins buffer" split into two 15-minute solving blocks, with the 20 minutes set aside for submission and chit checking. Adjust if the intended split differs.

## Team Member Roles During the Round

Each team has two members who split up once the solving window begins:

| Member | Task |
|--------|------|
| **Member 1** | Solves the Excel problem |
| **Member 2** | Finds the chits in the seminar halls and solves them, then writes the **final answer on the back of the chit** |

## Post-Round (Buffer Period)

- Teams submit their responses by mail or through the Google Form.
- Organisers check the chits returned by Member 2.
- The scoresheet members in rooms 402/403 record the results.
